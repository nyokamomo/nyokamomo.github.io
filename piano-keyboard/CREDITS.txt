45 medium-velocity recordings, every semitone E2–C6, selected across six strings.

钢片琴（实录）
Celeste Samples, recorded by stamperadam with spaced-pair Neumann KM-184s.
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source: https://freesound.org/people/stamperadam/packs/6166/
12 hard-struck stereo recordings from the public high-quality MP3 previews.
NYOKORE edits: map file names C2–G#5 to measured sounding pitch C4–G#7,
pitch correction, 80 Hz high-pass, level matching, short attack/end fades,
stereo 44.1 kHz 160 kbps MP3 encoding. No loops or synthesized replacement.

并非88个琴键逐音实录。当前网页采样库为单力度层。
