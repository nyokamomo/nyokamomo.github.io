The unmodified npm-distributed engine is served locally; its WebAssembly binary
is split into two byte ranges, reassembled without modification in the browser.
Pinned package URLs and SHA-256 checksums are in engine/manifest.json.
No imported video or generated audio is sent to the server.
Rebuild project assets: node scripts/prepare-video-mp3.mjs
