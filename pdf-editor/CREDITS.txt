Noto Sans SC, Noto Serif SC, Noto Sans JP, Noto Serif JP, Noto Sans KR and
Noto Serif KR Regular (SIL OFL 1.1): LICENSE-NotoSansSC.txt. These unmodified
region-specific subset OTFs come from the official Noto CJK repository:
https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
They are loaded only when selected and embedded once per used family during
PDF export. The font menu keeps Chinese, Japanese and Korean regional glyph
forms separate instead of relying on an operating-system font.
