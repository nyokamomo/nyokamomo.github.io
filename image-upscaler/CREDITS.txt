图像精扩仪 / NYOKORE

Neural super-resolution models: nunif / waifu2x, nagadomi.
https://github.com/nagadomi/nunif
Model sources and pinned hashes: assets.json
SwinUNet v3 art, SwinUNet photo and SwinUNet art_scan; 4x uses two 2x passes.
Tile geometry follows the MIT-licensed waifu2x implementation.
NYOKORE implements scanline blending, transparent RGB extension and bicubic alpha.
