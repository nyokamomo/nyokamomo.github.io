Nyokore modifications: selected a limited pitch range; original downloaded MP3 files
are unchanged. Runtime playback uses pitch shifting, gain adjustment and envelopes.
Cello sustain loops use a crossfade generated in memory; the synthesizer uses native
Web Audio oscillators. Attribution does not imply endorsement by the sample authors.
